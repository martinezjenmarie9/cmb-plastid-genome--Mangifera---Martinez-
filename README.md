 ## Plastome Summary
 
The *Mangifera sylvatica* plastome is a complete, circular chloroplast genome with a length of 157,824 bp. It has the typical quadripartite structure of plant plastomes, consisting of a large single-copy (LSC) region, a small single-copy (SSC) region, and two inverted repeat (IR) regions. The plastome contains genes involved in photosynthesis, gene expression, and other essential chloroplast functions.

## Genome Download, Upload, and Initial Analysis

The plastid genome of *Mangifera sylvatica* was obtained from the NCBI database using accession NC_057292. The genome sequence was downloaded in FASTA format and uploaded to the Galaxy platform as the dataset Mangifera_sylvatica_NC_057292.fasta. After uploading the genome, the Galaxy tool Fasta Statistics was used to calculate basic sequence statistics, including scaffold number, scaffold length, nucleotide composition, and other summary statistics. These results were used to assess the basic characteristics of the plastid genome before further analysis.

## Gene Content and Important Observations

The annotated *Mangifera sylvatica* plastid genome (NC_057292.1) is 157,824 bp in length. The genome contains 113 genes, including 79 protein-coding genes, 30 tRNA genes, and 4 rRNA genes. The plastome has the typical quadripartite structure consisting of a large single-copy (LSC) region, a small single-copy (SSC) region, and two inverted repeat (IR) regions. The GenBank record also contains annotations for gene coordinates and introns. No pseudogene annotation was identified in the record.
