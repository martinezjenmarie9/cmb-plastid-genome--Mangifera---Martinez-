### Software Used

**OGDRAW (OrganellarGenomeDRAW)** was used to generate the circular plastid genome map. OGDRAW is designed for visualizing annotated plastid and mitochondrial genomes from GenBank-format data. 

## OGDRAW Settings

The genome was displayed as a circular plastid genome map. The map includes the annotated gene features, gene orientation, the LSC, SSC, and inverted-repeat regions, as well as a GC-content graph. The standard gene-function color classification was used to distinguish different groups of genes. The map was exported as a PNG image.

## Main Structural Features

The *Mangifera sylvatica* plastid genome is a circular genome of 157,824 bp. The map shows the typical four-part plastid genome organization consisting of a large single-copy (LSC) region, small single-copy (SSC) region, and two inverted repeats (IRa and IRb). The genes are arranged around the circular genome and are shown with arrows indicating their transcriptional orientation. The map also displays different functional groups of genes, including photosynthesis-related genes, ATP synthase genes, ribosomal genes, transfer RNAs, and other plastid genes. The GC-content graph shows variation in GC content across different regions of the genome.
